MTEC-347 Sharing is Caring Proposal  
	My final project will be a 3–5 minute music piece performed using the MIDIMIX controller. I’m currently thinking of working within the electronica genre, but I’m also leaving room for exploration and experimentation, so the final style may evolve. On the MIDIMIX, I’ll use the sliders to control the volume of each sound, while the knobs will be used to adjust effects such as wet/dry levels and other parameters.
